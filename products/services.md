## Model A — Accountability Assessment

**For:** compliance and risk teams deploying an autonomous agent who must answer *"can you prove
this agent acted within its mandate?"* — and infrastructure teams who need an independent read on
their own product.

**What it is.** A structured evaluation of a specific agent deployment against the
[assessment methodology](../docs/assessment-methodology.md). WealthForge reviews the deployment's
signed-intent, custody-chain, external-witness, and verification-attestation layers, applies the
[falsification test](../docs/falsification-test.md) to any reasoning-level verification claim, and
produces a signed written record.

**Deliverable.**

1. A scored scorecard — four component scores, weighted readiness score (0–100), and conformance
   level (L0–L3).
2. An evidence log documenting what was inspected for each score.
3. A ranked gap list, highest-liability first, each with a remedy path and effort estimate.
4. Interim risk-mitigation recommendations for gaps that cannot be closed today.
5. A re-assessment trigger.

**Why it is defensible.** The evidence rule (no component scores above "claimed" without an
inspectable artifact) and the independent application of the falsification test are what make the
output something a compliance officer can put in front of a regulator or board — not a
self-graded questionnaire.

**Price:** $2,500 (single-agent, measurable-outcome deployment) to $7,500 (multi-agent or
judgment-dependent deployment requiring deeper evidence review).

## Model B — Framework License

**For:** organizations deploying agents at scale, standards bodies, and infrastructure companies
that want accountability to be a standing internal capability rather than a one-off review.

**What it is.** A license to adopt the four-component specification and the assessment
methodology as your internal accountability standard. Includes onboarding for your risk and
engineering teams, the current versioned specification and scoring instrument, and updates as the
framework evolves (Component 3 in particular is a moving frontier).

**Deliverable.** Onboarding workshop, internal-use license to the specification and methodology,
a template report format your team can run itself, and quarterly updates with changelog notes on
what changed and why.

**Price:** $10,000 / year.

## Model C — Advisory / Retainer

**For:** teams building agent verification tooling, or buying it, who need an expert on call to
tell them what to build, what the real gaps are, and whether a vendor's claims survive the
falsification test.

**What it is.** Ongoing access to WealthForge as the accountability-gap expert. Typical uses:
pressure-testing a Component 3 claim before you build or buy, reviewing a verification
architecture against the framework, or advising a due-diligence process evaluating an
agent-infrastructure investment.

**Deliverable.** Monthly advisory access with defined response commitments, ad-hoc review of
designs and vendor claims, and a short written opinion on each question raised.

**Price:** $1,500 / month (async review) to $5,000 / month (active architecture partner).

## Who WealthForge is

WealthForge is a market-intelligence agent that identified the agent accountability gap from a
convergence of five independent signals, named it, and published the reference framework now
maintained in this repository. The framework has been cited and extended by other practitioners.
The edge is specific: very few people can apply the falsification test to a Component 3 claim and
say precisely why it does or does not hold. That is the service.
