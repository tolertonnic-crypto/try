# Originality Checklist — per-video editorial requirements

> Shown for every item in the review queue. **A video cannot be approved until every
> box is checked by a human.** This is the channel's protection against YouTube's
> inauthentic-content policy: the January 2026 enforcement wave terminated templated,
> zero-judgment channels — the checklist is the proof of editorial judgment.

## Angle & originality

- [ ] The angle is distinct from every video in the last 20 uploads (the queue view
      shows the similarity report; anything flagged ≥ the threshold needs a rewrite
      or an explicit justification noted in the review log).
- [ ] The hook uses a different formula than the previous upload (formula name is
      recorded in the script metadata).
- [ ] The structure differs from the last 3 videos (structure name recorded in
      metadata; the generator rotates automatically, verify it did).
- [ ] This video would not exist in this form on another channel — name the editorial
      choice that makes it ours (framing, source, connection drawn).

## Factual integrity

- [ ] Every claim in the script's `factual_claims` list has been checked against at
      least one source **outside the script generator** (book, archive, encyclopedia,
      primary source). Mark each claim verified / softened / cut.
- [ ] Contested or legendary material is labeled as such in the narration.
- [ ] No invented quotes or dialogue.

## Human editorial pass

- [ ] A human read or listened to the full script and changed something — a cut, a
      reorder, a better sentence. (If nothing needed changing, note *why* it shipped
      as generated. "Looked fine" is not a note.)
- [ ] Title and thumbnail promise exactly what the video delivers.

## Compliance

- [ ] FTC disclosure present in the description (auto-enforced, verify visually).
- [ ] Affiliate links are relevant to this video's pillar, 3 max.
- [ ] Altered/synthetic content flag will be set on upload (auto — verify the queue
      item shows `synthetic: true` if AI voice or generated imagery is used).
- [ ] Nothing in the video, title, or tags fakes engagement or manipulates metrics.
